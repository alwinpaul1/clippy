Features

Your clipboard, on every device you own. Private, searchable, and quick to set up.

01 — Pairing

Scan once, and they stay paired.

Show a QR code on one device and scan it with the other. That's the whole setup: no account, no email, no password to forget.

  1. Install Clippy on two devices.
  2. Tap Add device and scan the code.
  3. Copy on either one. It shows up on the other in about a second.

02 — Encryption

The relay only sees ciphertext.

Every clip is sealed with AES-256 on the device that copied it. The key travels inside the QR code, never through the server, so the relay can deliver your clips but cannot read them.

  1. Encrypted before it leaves your device.
  2. The key is shared only by scanning, in person.
  3. Decrypted only on your own devices.

03 — Smart clips

It knows a code from a card number.

Clippy reads each clip on the device and offers the obvious next step. Nothing is sent anywhere to do it.

  1. Verification codes get a Copy 482913 button.
  2. Card numbers and IBANs are hidden and wiped after a few minutes.
  3. Search finds "hauptbanhof" even when you typed it wrong.
What else

The small things that make it worth keeping.

Pin what matters

Pinned clips stay at the top and never drop off the history.

Filter by device

One tap shows only what came from your phone, or only from the PC.

Sensitive auto-clear

Card numbers and IBANs are removed from every device after 1, 5 or 15 minutes.

Images too

Screenshots and photos sync in their original format, not re-compressed.

Search that forgives typos

Type "otp", "card" or "link" to find clips by what they are.

One-tap actions

Call a number, open a link, email an address or map a street, straight from the clip.

Android quick tile

Send what you just copied from the notification shade, even from apps that block clipboard access.

Updates itself

A card tells you when a new version is out. One tap installs it on every platform.

No account, ever

No sign-up, no email, no tracking inside the app. Your devices are the only login.

FAQ

Things you'll probably ask.

Can the server read my clips?

No. Clips are encrypted with AES-256 on your device before they are sent, and the key only exists on devices you paired by scanning the QR code. The relay stores and forwards ciphertext.

Is there an iPhone or Linux version?

Not yet. Clippy runs on macOS 12 or later, Android 6 or later, and 64-bit Windows 10 or later.

How long are clips kept?

The 25 most recent clips stay in your shared history, plus anything you pin. Delete a clip on one device and it disappears from all of them. Card numbers and IBANs clear themselves after the time you choose.

Why does Android ask for accessibility access?

Android stops background apps from reading the clipboard. The accessibility service is how Clippy notices a copy while you're in another app. It reads nothing else. Without it you can still send clips with the quick tile.

Why is the Android app an APK and not on Google Play?

Clippy is a free side project and ships directly from this site. The APK is signed with one permanent key, and the app updates itself in place when a new version is out.

Does it cost anything?

No. It's free and MIT licensed, and the source is on GitHub.
